This policy explains how the Lilium Kelloggii posting app ("the App") handles information.

1. Information we use

When you connect TikTok, the App receives basic profile information (such as your display name and avatar) and an access token that lets it upload and publish videos you choose.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to post and manage your own content on your request. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely and used only for the App's posting features.

4. Your choices

You can disconnect the App at any time in your TikTok settings, and you can ask us to delete your data by emailing us.

5. Children

The App is not meant for children under 13.
